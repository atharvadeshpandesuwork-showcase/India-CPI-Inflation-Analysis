# India-CPI-Inflation-Analysis
Comprehensive analysis of India's CPI Inflation data (2017-2023) across Rural, Urban, and combined sectors. This project investigates inflation trends category-wise CPI contributions, food inflation drivers, COVID-19 impacts and the relationship between crude oil and price fluctuations and consumer inflation using Excel and Power Point

# Overview
This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) data from 2017 to 2023 to understand inflation trends accross Rural, Urban and Coombined sectors.

The analysis focuses on:
- CPI basket contribution by broader categories.
- Year-over-Year inflation trends.
- Food Inflation Analysis.
- Impact of covid-19 on inflation.
- Relationship between crude oil prices and inflation.

Analyze the impact of key pandemic milestone (first lockdown) on CPI % specifically focus on categories like healthcare, food and essential services. - Identify trends in oil price change with change in inflations prices of all the categories and identify category whose inflation prices strongly changes with fluctuations in imported oil price.

### 2. CPI Basket Contribution Analysis

Calculated the contribution of each broader category toward the overall CPI basket for Rural, Urban, and Combined Sectors.

### Key Findings

Rural + Urban (Combined)

| Category | Contribution (Combined) |
|-----------|-----------|
| Basic Food Items | 35% |
| Services & Miscellaneous | 23% |
| Other Food & Intoxicants | 19% |
| Clothing & Footwear | 12% |
| Housing & Utilities | 11% |

**Insight:** Basic Food items emerged as the largest contributor to Indias CPI basket.

### 3. Year-over-Year Inflation Trend Analysis

Analyzed annual inflation growth rates from 2017 onwards for the Combined CPI basket.

#### Key Findings

- Highest inflation observed in **2019**
- Lowest inflation observed in **2023**

#### Major Drivers Behind 2019 Inflation

- Sharp rise in onion and vegetable prices
- Food supply shortages
- Lower agricultural output
- Rising crude oil prices
- Higher transportation costs
- Global commodity price uncertainty caused by the US-China trade war

### 5. COVID-19 Impact Analysis

Compared inflation trends before and after the COVID-19 pandemic.

#### Time Periods

- Pre-COVID: 2017–2020
- Post-COVID: 2020–2023

| Category | Change |
|-----------|-----------|
| Transport & Communication | +3.42 |
| Food Products Combined | +0.87 |
| General Index | +0.38 |
| Education | -2.52 |
| Health | -0.55 |

#### Key Insights

- Transport & Communication recorded the largest increase after COVID.
- Education recorded the largest decline.
- Overall inflation increased after the pandemic.

### 6. Imported Crude Oil Price Analysis

Analyzed imported crude oil prices between 2021 and 2023.

### Metrics Calculated

- Monthly Unit Price
- Month-over-Month Growth Rate
- Price Volatility

#### Key Findings

| Metric | Value |
|----------|----------|
| Highest Increase | 18.31% (Mar-22) |
| Largest Decline | -9.52% (Dec-22) |
| Peak Volatility | 2022 |

#### Observations

- Oil prices rose sharply during early 2022.
- Sustained declines were observed throughout late 2022 and early 2023.
- Crude oil price fluctuations influenced multiple CPI categories.

---

### 7. Correlation Analysis

Performed correlation analysis between imported crude oil price movements and CPI inflation categories.

#### Strongest Positive Correlations

| Category | Correlation |
|-----------|-----------|
| Meat & Fish | 0.58 |
| Oils & Fats | 0.52 |
| Food & Beverages | 0.34 |

#### Key Insight

Food-related categories showed the strongest relationship with crude oil price fluctuations, indicating transportation and supply-chain effects on consumer prices.

## Key Business Insights

- Basic Food Items contribute over one-third of India's CPI basket.
- Food inflation remains the primary driver of overall inflation.
- Spices and cereals experienced the highest inflation growth.
- Oils & Fats and Vegetables experienced significant deflation.
- COVID-19 increased transportation-related inflation substantially.
- Crude oil price volatility had measurable effects on several CPI categories.
- Inflation patterns differed across Rural and Urban sectors.
